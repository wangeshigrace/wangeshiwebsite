The Story

How it happened

It wasn't planned. It started with curiosity and a lot of late nights.

1

The Beginning

I was bored and curious

I stumbled onto ChatGPT and started asking random questions. I didn't know it would change everything. I just wanted to see what it could do.

2

First Experiment

I asked it to teach me HTML

I typed "teach me how to build a website from scratch" and it actually did. Step by step. I built my first page that same week. It was messy, but it worked.

3

Getting Creative

Design, logos, graphics

I found Canva AI and image tools for visuals. I started designing brand assets, social media content, and ideas that looked more polished than what I could do alone.

4

Level Up

AI became my creative partner

With the right prompts and repetition, I could build faster, test ideas, and learn by doing. What felt technical became something I could actually practice every day.

5

Today

Real work, real results

I now use AI to support websites, content, branding, and strategy. It didn't replace my effort - it made my effort go further.
